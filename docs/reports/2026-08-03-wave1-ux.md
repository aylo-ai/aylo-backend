## 2. Trust moments — what exists vs. what's needed

**The single largest gap: there is no integration-health surface at all.**
`src/lib/integrations-data.ts` models a channel as only `connected` + `active`.
On the backend, `apps/integration/models.py:30-31` stores `api_token` /
`refresh_token` with **no expiry column**, and
`apps/integration/gateways/instagram.py:43-52` (`instagram_refresh_token`) has
**zero callers** — it is invoked once, fire-and-forget, at connect time
(`instagram.py:39`, result discarded), and no Celery beat schedule exists.
Instagram long-lived tokens last 60 days. **Auto-replies will silently stop
~60 days after every connect, and neither the backend nor the UI can tell the
user.** The status indicator the brief asks for cannot be built until the
backend stores `token_expires_at` and refreshes on a schedule.

Also missing at the OAuth consent step: any statement of what we access or
never do. `InstagramConnectDrawer.tsx:36-66` is entirely mechanical
instructions. Disconnect (`:122-125`) says "you can't undo" but says nothing
about what happens to stored customer messages — and **unlink fires with no
confirmation** (`IntegrationsView.tsx:279-283`, `:424-429`) despite
`components/ui/ConfirmInline.tsx` already existing.

## 5. Prioritized redesign list

1. **Mobile navigation escape hatch** (P0) — link the wordmark, add a section
   switcher to the drawer. One afternoon; removes a hard dead end.
2. **Primary button contrast → `brand-700`** (P0). One token change, product-wide.
3. **Token lifecycle, backend + UI** (P0) — `token_expires_at`, day-45 refresh
   task, status chip, expiring/revoked banners. The largest silent-failure risk.
4. **Confirm before unlink** (P0), with the data-retention sentence above.
5. **i18n extraction sweep** (P0/P1) — integrations and chats first; add `uz-Cyrl`.
6. **Message delivery states + retry** in the inbox (P1).
7. **Mobile drawer a11y** — reuse `Drawer` (P1).
8. **Consent block + rename "Agent Answer"** (P1).
9. **"Send a test DM" verification step** after connect (P2).
10. **Consolidate empty/loading/error onto `EmptyState`/`ErrorState`** (P2).
